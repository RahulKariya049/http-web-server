About the Journal

Why Math Behind Machines?

"Math Behind Machines explores the mathematical ideas powering computer science through technical articles and beautiful visualizations."
Every system we rely on — a search engine, a game, an encrypted message, a distributed database — is a piece of mathematics wearing an engineer's clothes. We write essays that pull that mathematics back into view: the proofs, the transforms, the geometry, and the theory that make computing possible in the first place.
